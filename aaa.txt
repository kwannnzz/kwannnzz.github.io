Revenue cut-off / omitted revenue	Increase taxable income	Permanent unless timing	"Propose when goods ownership has transferred or service is completed in the tax year but revenue is not booked/taxed. Quantify gross revenue omitted, reconciled to contracts, delivery/customs records, acceptance minutes, invoices and VAT returns."	"CIT revenue follows the prescribed recognition point, not merely invoice/accounting timing. Deduct in a later year only if the same revenue is subsequently booked and would otherwise be taxed twice."	Article 8 Decree 320; Article 5 Circular 78; Article 3 Circular 96.	"Sales contract/Incoterms, delivery note, customs declaration, acceptance record, VAT invoice/return, GL–FS–VAT reconciliation."
Unqualified sales return / trade discount / price reduction	Increase taxable income	Permanent or temporary	"Add back the reduction if programme/policy, eligibility condition, minutes, adjusted invoice or other support is missing. Payment discount should be tested as an expense under B4.4; promotion-like discount goes to B4.5."	A reduction is accepted only when its commercial basis and documents prove the customer entitlement and amount. Reverse only when valid documents are subsequently issued and the item remains claimable.	Revenue guidance above; Point d9 Clause 1 Article 9 Decree 320.	"Sales policy/programme, customer eligibility calculation, return/discount minutes, adjusted invoice, credit note, approval."
Expenses matched to revenue decreased under B9	Increase taxable income	Temporary/matching	"When current-year revenue is deducted in B9 because it was taxed previously, remove the directly corresponding current-year expense from deduction. Quantify traceable COGS/direct costs for that revenue."	Prevents deducting cost in a year where the related revenue is excluded. Do not adjust qualifying stand-alone expenses specifically allowed by Point i Clause 2 Article 9 Decree 320.	Point i Clause 2 Article 9 Decree 320; revenue–expense matching guidance in B3i.	"B9 schedule, prior-year CIT return, sales/COGS mapping, inventory movement and cost allocation."
"Depreciation—non-business, no ownership/use support, disposed/idle assets"	Increase taxable income	Mainly permanent	"Add back depreciation/NBV for assets not used for business, not legally owned/used, already disposed, still usable but destroyed, or unsupported. Idle land/facilities may require adjustment unless business use/investment plan is evidenced."	"Tax deduction requires business purpose, legal right and support. Damaged assets no longer usable may remain deductible if destruction and condition are properly documented; assets available for lease without tenants may qualify."	Point 6 Article 10 Decree 320; Circular 45/2013/TT-BTC; OL 655/TCT-CS dated 14 Feb 2025 for land-related review.	"FAR, ownership/LURC/LLA, licence/IRC, location and usage evidence, disposal/destruction minutes, photos, approvals."
"Depreciation—wrong historical cost, useful life, start date or calculation"	Increase or decrease taxable income	Temporary	Recalculate tax depreciation asset-by-asset. Add back over-claimed depreciation; deduct under-claimed amount where eligible. Track prior-year temporary adjustments to avoid duplication.	"Only depreciation calculated on eligible cost, prescribed useful life and correct in-use date is deductible; timing differences reverse over the remaining tax life."	Circular 45/2013/TT-BTC; Point 6 Article 10 Decree 320.	"FAR, invoices/contracts, capitalization documents, commissioning date, useful-life classification, prior-year roll-forward."
Passenger cars ?9 seats—cost above VND1.6bn	Increase taxable income	Permanent	"For non-qualifying cars, add back depreciation attributable to historical cost exceeding VND1.6bn; apply the specific exceptions, if any, based on business nature."	The excess cost base is not accepted for CIT depreciation under the template review procedure.	Point 6 Article 10 Decree 320 and Circular 45/2013/TT-BTC as cited in B4.1i.	"Vehicle registration, invoice, FAR, business-use assessment and depreciation calculation."
Prepayments / repairs / pre-establishment and pre-operation costs	Increase or decrease taxable income	Temporary	Add back amounts expensed faster than permitted or lacking business purpose/support. Recalculate reasonable allocation: qualifying leased-asset renovation/repair generally allocated within 3 years; pre-establishment costs capitalised and allocated within 3 years; asset-forming pre-operation costs capitalised.	Deduction follows the permitted allocation period and nature. Reverse through deductible amortisation in later years; other qualifying pre-operation costs may be deductible when incurred.	Point i5 Clause 2 Article 9 Decree 320; Point 3 Article 3 Circular 45; Circular 45/2013/TT-BTC.	"Expense listing, lease contract, invoices, completion minutes, allocation schedule, business-purpose support."
Destroyed/damaged/expired/obsolete inventory and disposal costs	Increase taxable income	Permanent if unqualified	Add back book value and destruction costs when reason is outside qualifying cases or statutory records are incomplete. No adjustment where qualifying deterioration/obsolescence/expiry/discontinuation is fully documented.	CIT deduction depends on both an eligible cause and sufficient destruction/supporting documentation; provisions are excluded from destruction cost.	Point 2.1 Article 4 Circular 96; Point g and i7 Clause 2 Article 9 Decree 320; draft circular references remain placeholders in template.	"Inventory list, condition/reason evidence, destruction decision and minutes, participant records, photos, invoices for disposal, insurance/compensation."
Stocktake loss	Increase taxable income	Permanent unless offset justified	Add back unexplained/unqualified stocktake shortage. A shortage demonstrably linked to a taxable stocktake gain may be offset against that gain.	Unsubstantiated physical loss is not an eligible business cost; only the net supported exposure should be adjusted.	Point 2.1 Article 4 Circular 96; Point g Clause 2 Article 9 Decree 320.	"Stocktake minutes, reconciliation to inventory ledger, investigation/approval, gain-offset mapping, compensation/insurance."
Materials/fuel/power/goods above statutory consumption norms	Increase taxable income	Permanent	Compare actual consumption with Government-prescribed industry norms and add back the excess. Also assess unjustified standard-cost/COGS system adjustments and costs allocated to unsold inventory.	Costs exceeding mandatory consumption norms or overstating current-year COGS are not deductible in the current period.	Point 2.3 Article 4 Circular 96 plus applicable sector regulations listed in B4.2i.	"Production records, BOM/norms, output data, utility/fuel logs, sector rules, standard-to-actual variance and inventory allocation."
Interest rate above permitted cap for non-credit-institution/individual lender	Increase taxable income	Permanent	Calculate non-deductible interest = interest charge × excess-rate ratio. Apply the relevant cap identified by the template (Civil Code 20% p.a.; template also references 1.5× SBV prime rate wording).	The portion above the legal borrowing-rate limit is not deductible.	Point 2.17 Article 4 Circular 96; Clause 10 Article 10 Decree 320; Article 468 Civil Code 2015.	"Loan contract, lender status, drawdown and repayment, rate calculation, interest ledger, SBV/reference rate where applicable."
Offshore loan not registered with SBV when required	Increase taxable income	Permanent / arguable per template	Add back interest for medium/long-term or extended/outstanding short-term foreign loans requiring registration where registration is absent/insufficient.	Non-compliance with mandatory foreign-loan registration exposes the interest deduction to challenge.	Circular 12/2022/TT-NHNN; Circular 08/2023/TT-NHNN; Circular 19/2024/TT-NHNN.	"Loan agreement/amendments, SBV registration/confirmation, withdrawal and repayment schedule, outstanding balance ageing."
Interest corresponding to under-contributed charter capital	Increase taxable income	Permanent	"If loan ? under-contributed capital, consider total related interest; otherwise allocate interest by under-contributed capital/total loans or calculate by shortfall × rate × shortfall period, consistent with facts."	Borrowing cost that finances a capital contribution shortfall is not deductible during the non-compliance period.	Clause 9 Article 10 Decree 320; Point 2.18 Article 4 Circular 96.	"IRC/ERC/charter, capital contribution schedule and bank evidence, loan balances, daily/monthly interest computation."
Interest corresponding to interest-free onward loans	Increase taxable income; possibly deemed income	Permanent / arguable	"If the company incurred interest and granted an interest-free loan, allocate the borrowing cost corresponding to the free loan and add it back. If no interest cost was incurred, assess deemed taxable income under B7."	The funded amount does not serve the company’s income-generating business on arm’s-length terms.	Business-purpose rule and B4.3i practice guidance; discuss judgment with MIC/PIC.	"Borrowing and onward-loan contracts, fund-flow tracing, balances, rates, related-party status and business rationale."
Net interest exceeding 30% NOPBID for related-party transactions	Increase taxable income	Temporary/carry-forward subject to rules	"Compute net interest = interest expense less interest income; add back excess over 30% of NOPBID. If NOPBID <0, template proposes adjustment of net interest and flags carry-forward risk/conditions."	"Related-party interest deduction is capped; usable excess must be tracked by incurring year and available capacity, avoiding double adjustment with accruals/capitalised interest."	Point c Clause 4 Article 10 Decree 320; Decree 132 referenced by B4.3i.	"RPT assessment, P&L mapping, interest income/expense, depreciation, NOPBID calculation and carry-forward roll-forward."
Interest during investment/construction phase	Increase taxable income now; depreciate later	Temporary	"Add back interest expensed during asset construction that should be capitalised, net of eligible deposit interest. Allocate mixed-purpose borrowing based on a supportable method."	"Cost directly attributable to forming the asset belongs in investment value, then becomes deductible through depreciation/amortisation."	Point 2.31 Article 4 Circular 96; Clause 18 Article 10 Decree 320.	"Project timeline, CIP ledger, loan purpose, fund tracing, interest/deposit income and capitalization calculation."
Missing valid invoice/support or non-cash payment evidence	Increase taxable income	Permanent unless cured	"Add back unsupported expenses. For payments from VND5m VAT-inclusive, test non-cash evidence under the FY25 template effective 15 Dec 2025, including aggregation of invoices from one supplier in one day and employee reimbursement documentation."	"Deduction requires legitimate documents and prescribed payment evidence. If defects are later validly cured, reassess timing and legal eligibility before reversing."	Article 9 CIT Law 2025; Clause 1 Article 9 Decree 320/2025; Decrees 123/2020 and 70/2025 on e-invoices; Circular 96 legacy VND20m rule.	"Contract/PO, invoice, acceptance, bank voucher, employee authorization/policy, reimbursement trail and supplier-day aggregation."
"Administrative penalties, back taxes and non-business expenses"	Increase taxable income	Permanent	"Add back administrative penalties and back-tax amounts charged to PBT, plus costs lacking a demonstrable business nexus. Check gifts/donations, payment-on-behalf and output/input VAT booked to expense separately."	These items are expressly non-deductible or fail the business-purpose condition.	Point a Clause 2 Article 9 CIT Law 2025; Clause 2 and Article 10 Decree 320/2025; OL 11238/BTC-CST dated 12 Aug 2014.	"Penalty/tax decisions, GL scans, invoices/contracts, business-purpose explanation, payment-on-behalf agreements."
Related-party / intra-group service or royalty without benefit and substance	Increase taxable income	Permanent	"Add back fees where service provides no direct benefit, duplicates shareholder/group activity, is a pass-through without value-add, is not arm’s length, or lacks evidence of delivery/IP ownership. Quantify by unsupported service/royalty line."	"A deductible charge must evidence actual service, benefit, capability, arm’s-length pricing and legal IP rights."	Decree 20 references in B4.4i; general Article 9/10 Decree 320 conditions; royalty documentation guidance in B4.4i.	"Agreement, deliverables, emails/timesheets, benefit test, allocation key, TP support, FCT, IP/technology ownership documents."
Major repair / fixed-asset upgrade expensed immediately	Increase taxable income then deduct by allocation/depreciation	Temporary	Add back current-year expense that creates/extends asset benefit and should be capitalised or allocated; calculate subsequent tax depreciation/amortisation.	The cost is not wholly attributable to the current period; timing follows capitalization/allocation rules.	B4.4i procedure; cross-reference Circular 45 and B4.1i.	"Scope/contract, technical assessment, acceptance minutes, capitalization threshold and allocation/depreciation schedule."
"Marketing, promotion, customer/distributor support"	Increase taxable income if unqualified	Mainly permanent	"Add back campaigns/support lacking required DoIT notification/registration, contract, invoice or proof of eligibility/delivery. "The commercial programme, participant conditions, value and invoicing must align; unconditional fixed discount may be treated as promotion." "The deduction is conditional on qualifying activity and prescribed documentation, not merely the account description." Accrued sales reduction and judgmental accrual categories	Increase taxable income or note risk	Temporary / arguable	"Normally add back accrued sales reduction until invoice is issued.
